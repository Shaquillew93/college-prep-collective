FAQs

Common questions

Academic integrity

Our academic writing services focus on coaching, proofreading, formatting, and feedback. Students remain the authors of their work. We do not write assignments, theses, or dissertations on a student’s behalf, produce research findings, or provide editing that violates institutional policies. Students are responsible for confirming the editing rules of their program.

Do you work with students outside high school?

Yes. College, transfer, graduate, and doctoral students are welcome.

Can you write my dissertation or paper for me?

No. The student retains authorship. Permissible formatting, proofreading, and coaching are available.

Do you offer monthly tutoring?

Yes. Recurring virtual English and writing instruction for grades 8 to 12 is available, subject to capacity.

Are consultations included?

Package scope varies. One-time advising sessions may be booked separately.

Do you guarantee admission or scholarship awards?

No. Guidance improves preparation but does not guarantee outcomes.
